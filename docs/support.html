Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use Budget Tracker?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. You open the app and start budgeting. Everything you enter is stored privately on your own device.

Where is my data stored?

All of your budgets, income, categories, and transactions are saved locally on your device. Nothing is sent to a server, and we cannot see your data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I set up my budget?

On first launch, the app walks you through a short setup: your income, your priorities, and your savings. From there, the Home screen shows what's safe to spend today, and you can log spending in a few taps.

How do reminders work?

Reminders are optional. If you turn them on in Settings, the app schedules local notifications on your device — for example, a daily check-in and a month-end reminder. They are generated entirely on your device and can be turned off at any time.

How do I reset or delete my data?

Go to Settings → Reset inside the app to clear your data, or simply uninstall the app, which removes everything stored locally.

Which currency does it use?

You choose your currency symbol during setup, so the app works wherever you are.
